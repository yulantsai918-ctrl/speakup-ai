Page 3 • PRE-TRIP CALIBRATION

英文思維的「道地校準」

教科書直翻 (Textbook English)

猶豫不決時：

I don't know (太僵硬)

點餐/購物時：

I want this (像命令)

嫌太貴時：

It's too expensive (顯得唐突)

聽不懂時：

What did you say? (語氣粗魯)
都會道地美語 (Authentic Street English)

道地句型：

I'm on the fence / I'm still deciding right now

道地句型：

Can I get... / I'll go with...

道地句型：

It's a little pricey

道地句型：

Sorry, I didn't catch that / Can you say that one more time?
核心觀念： 拋棄「命令式、絕對化」的字面直翻，改用「保留空間」的道地軟語氣 (Softening Tone)，才能完美過渡到真實美國社交環境。
Page 4 • TRANSIT & COMMUTE

城市通勤導航：從地鐵到共乘

公共運輸 (Transit)

確認路線： "Does this train go downtown?"
轉乘支付： "Do I tap again when transferring?"

(No, it counts as one ride)

確認公車： "Is this the right bus to the airport?"

共享搭乘 (Rideshare / Uber)

上車確認： "Are you here for [Name]?"
空調與偏好： "Could you turn the AC up?" / "I'm okay with whichever route is faster."
司機與乘客寒暄 (Small Talk)： "Traffic was brutal this morning."
→ "Yeah, Mondays always hit hard."
Page 5 • ORDERING FORMULA

模組化點餐：打造你的客製化公式

1. 開場 (The Ask)
2. 基底 (The Base)
3. 客製 (Customs)
4. 附加 (Extras)
實戰組裝完成金句：

"Can I get a medium iced latte with oat milk, half sweet, and add an extra shot, to go, please."

Page 6 • CHECKOUT BATTLE

結帳攻防戰：預判店員的連環問題

人工結帳通道 (Cashier Checkout)

店員起手式：

  • "Did you find everything you needed today?"
  • "Do you have a rewards account here?"
  • "Paper or plastic today?" (要紙袋還是塑膠袋)
  • "Will you be paying with card or cash today?"

你可以直接高效率回應：

  • "I brought my own bag. I'll pay with card."
  • "Just these items for today."

自助結帳台 (Self-Checkout)

機台語音提示與問題：

  • "Please scan your item."
  • "Unexpected item in the bagging area." (裝袋區有未知物品，常見警報)

尋求人工協助的金句：

  • "This item is not scanning properly."
  • "It rang up higher than I expected." (刷出來的金額比標價高)

Page 8 • CONVERSATION LOOP

讓對話永不落地的「推進迴圈」

Step 1

回應並展現態度 (Answer)

不要只回答 Yes/No。多加一句具體感受：
"I'm doing well, thanks for asking."
"It's been pretty chill, honestly."

Step 2

拋回問題或延伸 (Ask Back)

將對話球回拋：
"What's up with you today?"
"What do you usually do for fun?"

Step 3

情緒價值與共鳴 (React)

表示理解與附和：
"Oh wow, that's actually crazy!"
"Yeah, I completely agree with that."

跳出迴圈 (Escape Route)： 聊夠了想優雅離開？使用："I'm going to grab a drink real quick." 或 "I'll catch you later."
Page 9 • OFFICE NAVIGATION

辦公室導航：進度確認與專業溝通

專案進度與釐清 (Projects)

確認進度： "How's the presentation coming along?" / "Are you close to wrapping things up?"
釐清誤會： "Could you explain the process again? I might be misunderstanding this task."
提出建議： "I actually have another suggestion here. What if we shortened the process?"

請假與下班 (Time-off & Wrapping Up)

請求請假： "I'd like to request next Monday off." / "Would leaving early today be possible?"
下班宣告： "Are you wrapping things up already? That's good enough for one day. I'm heading out for tonight."

Page 11 • PHARMACY & CLINIC

醫療急診救援：精準描述你的痛覺

痛覺等級與描述法

"It feels more like a dull ache." (隱隱作痛)

Page 12 • EMERGENCY COPE

尷尬時刻救生圈：聽不懂、腦袋空白怎麼辦？

1. 爭取時間 (Buying Time)

當你需要組織詞彙時，請不要發出尷尬的 "Uh..."，改用：

  • "That's a good question, honestly."
  • "Let me think for just a second."
  • "I'm not totally sure right now."
2. 請求重述/澄清

沒聽懂千萬不要裝懂點頭，勇敢禮貌地要求重述：

  • "Sorry, I didn't catch that."
  • "Wait, what did you mean by that?"
  • "Can you say that one more time?"
3. 切換話題 (Pivoting)

話題卡住或想轉移焦點時，可以順暢自然地切換：

  • "Anyway, what were you saying earlier?"
  • "By the way, did you see that..."
Page 13 • SERVICE RECOVERY

溫和且堅定：餐飲與購物的客訴應對

Step 1. 點出問題 (Stating)

不帶攻擊性地指出事實：

  • "I think this isn't what I ordered."
  • "It rang up higher than I expected."
  • "I think we're missing the fries."
Step 2. 提出訴求 (Requesting)

清晰表達你需要的解決方案：

  • "Would it be possible to redo this?"
  • "Could I switch this to a salad?"
  • "I'd like to request a refund."
Step 3. 緩和語氣 (Softening)

展現禮貌與同理心：

  • "I'm sorry to bother you."
  • "Not at all, I understand."
Page 14 • THE ALGORITHM

沉浸式語言環境的底層邏輯

1. 情境預期 (Anticipation)

道地英文不是靠「聽力」硬扛，而是靠「預判」。例如：在走到結帳櫃檯前，大腦已預期對方會問 Paper or plastic / Insert or tap，藉此提早準備。

2. 掌握模組 (Modularity)

不要每次都費力重新造句。將點餐、寒暄、客訴視為「公式（如 Slide 5）」，臨場只需要抽換關鍵字，即可流暢應答。

3. 勇敢救場 (Recovery)

母語人士也會結巴、聽不懂。掌握爭取時間的填充句（Let me think for a second），允許自己犯錯，讓對話能夠持續流動才是真正重點。
